## Questions

## Basic

* Compare the average final grade (G3) of school GP and school MS.
* Calculate the average final grade for each study time level in both schools.
* Compare the average final grade of students with and without family educational support in both schools.
* Compare the average absences and average final grade of both schools.
* Compare the average final grade of urban and rural students in both schools.
* Compare the average final grade of students with and without extracurricular activities in both schools.

## Advanced

* Group students into Low, Medium and High alcohol consumption (weekday plus weekend) using `CASE WHEN`, then compare their average final grades in both schools.
